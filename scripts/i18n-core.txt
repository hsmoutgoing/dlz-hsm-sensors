122	Industries — Oil & Gas, Water, Chemical, Power, Food
123	How HSM industrial sensors are specified for oil and gas, chemical, water treatment, power generation, food and beverage, and pharmaceutical duties — including hazardous area, sanitary and high-pressure variants.
124	Separators, storage tanks, gathering lines and tank farms. The recurring requirements are hazardous-area certification, methane detection at a distance, and level instruments that keep working when the medium coats the sensor.
125	Leak detection on long lines
126	A TDLAS methane detector measures absorption along the beam instead of at a point, so a patrol can sweep a flange stack or valve station from several metres away and find the leak without entering the hazard zone.
127	Interface and product level in separators
128	Magnetostrictive transmitters give continuous 4–20 mA or RS485 over 3–5 m of stroke with no moving parts in the vessel. A magnetic flip-column gauge alongside gives a local readout that needs no power at all.
129	Pump protection on transfer duty
130	A dry-running pump destroys itself in minutes. Thermal flow switches with no moving part in the flow path hold their set-point where a paddle switch would jam on wax or scale.

293	Dry contact / relay
294	Switching a pump, valve, alarm or interlock directly. No controller required.

296	HWS, HLS, HTS float and magnetic switches, flow switches, pressure controllers

300	HSM flow switches, UB ultrasonic sensors, HSM41 laser displacement
301	4–20 mA (2-wire)
302	Long cable runs into a PLC analogue card or DCS. Immune to volt-drop over distance.
303	The loop needs a minimum supply voltage that rises with the total loop resistance.
304	PDD pressure transmitters, HPS submersible level, TPT temperature transmitters
305	0–10 V / 1–5 V
306	Short runs inside a cabinet, or into a controller with voltage inputs.
307	Voltage signals degrade over distance and pick up noise. Keep the run short.

315	Battery life depends on reporting interval and signal strength; confirm both before sizing.
316	Wireless pressure, temperature, level and gas instruments
317	Float switch
318	A discrete point — pump start/stop, high alarm. No power needed for the switch itself.

502	Thermal flow switches and closed-loop flow controllers for pipe networks
503	Thermal-dispersion flow switches detect whether liquid or gas in a pipe is moving above a set point, then switch a relay. There is no paddle, no vane and no moving part in the flow path, so debris and scale do not jam the instrument. Our range covers the standard single set-point switch, a dual set-point version for alarm-and-trip duty, and a digital model that shows live flow velocity on an LED display. All three are fully sealed and rated IP67, with a wetted probe in SUS 316L. Where you need regulation rather than detection, the S400 controller closes the loop with a proportional valve.

509	Level
510	Float, magnetic, capacitive, submersible and magnetostrictive level measurement
511	Liquid level is the widest part of our catalogue because no single principle suits every tank. Float switches give a dry contact at one or more discrete points and need no power. Magnetic level gauges read directly from a red-and-white flip column — no electronics at all — and can add a 4–20 mA transmitter for remote indication. Magnetic-induction and magnetostrictive transmitters give continuous 4–20 mA or RS485 output over 3 to 5 metres. Submersible transmitters convert hydrostatic head into a signal and suit deep wells and reservoirs. Capacitive models handle viscous and coating media. Where foam, build-up or aggressive chemistry rules out contact sensing, the microwave switch detects through the wall of the vessel.

521	Our pressure line splits into two jobs. For pure measurement, the PDD series covers differential pressure from ±100 Pa up to 40 MPa with 0.2 or 0.5 accuracy class, a 40:1 turndown and a two-wire 4–20 mA output. For measurement plus local control, the PCD and PCC controllers add a bright LED display, configurable alarm points set from the keypad, and four or five relay outputs that can trip pumps, valves or alarms directly — no separate PLC needed for a small skid. Both families share stainless-steel housings, IP65 protection and standard G1/2 or M20×1.5 process connections, with custom threads available.

537	Non-contact distance sensing from 120 mm to 4 m, unaffected by colour or gloss
538	Ultrasonic sensors time the echo of a sound pulse, so they detect clear glass, polished metal, black rubber and liquid surfaces that optical sensors miss. The UB series covers seven sensing ranges from 120 mm to 4000 mm in M12, M18 and M30 threaded barrels, all with PNP or NPN switching output and an adjustable window. Because the measurement is acoustic rather than optical, dust, mist and ambient light do not degrade it. Typical duty is presence detection on a conveyor, fill-level checking in a bin, loop control on a web, and collision avoidance on AGVs.

546	TDLAS methane detectors for pipelines, confined spaces and unmanned inspection
547	These instruments use tunable diode laser absorption spectroscopy (TDLAS) to measure methane along the line of sight rather than at a single point. A laser is aimed at the target surface and the reflected beam is analysed, so a leak several tens of metres away is detected without walking into the hazard. Response is 0.06 s on the wearable unit, which matters when a patrol is moving. The range includes a helmet-mounted detector for walk-around inspection, a long-range handheld, and an explosion-proof pan-tilt head for fixed installation on a gantry or tank farm.
